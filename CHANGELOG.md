## [0.1.0] — 2026-11-05

### Release characterization

Initial **research-framework snapshot** for WE-SIO. It proposes a contract-first, perspective-aware way to represent and locate world entities, attach lightweight structural intelligence, preserve unresolved residuals, and evolve hypotheses, definitions, and research interfaces. The repository is intended as a Collective Learning relay artifact.

**Evidence status:** Proposed / E0 unless an individual document explicitly supplies a higher-grade, independently checkable result. No WE-HDT performance advantage, scientific discovery, clinical effectiveness, or consciousness-related conclusion is asserted as experimentally established.

### Added — conceptual architecture

- **World Entity (WE)** as an entity-oriented research abstraction with identity distinct from representations, metric signatures, source records, and physical indexes.
- **Perspective descriptors** with typed semantics, domains, units where applicable, uncertainty, provenance, time, and versioning.
- **WE Hybrid Differential Tree (WE-HDT)** as a proposed localization architecture joining candidate retrieval with evidence-aware resolution; Euclidean-first is an optional strategy, not a universal prerequisite.
- **Perspective-Adaptive Variable Blocks (PAVB)** and **Adaptive Variable-Intelligence Blocks (AVIB)** as separate, potentially co-evolving axes of partitioning and intelligence allocation.
- **Metric Leaf Intelligence Unit (MLIU)** with six operations: Locate, Resolve, Relate, Infer, Question, and Evolve; logical metric leaves need not correspond to physical tree leaves.
- **Two-Ways CCC** for forward accountable evaluation and reverse residual-driven questioning, subject to typed cross-perspective contracts.
- **Residual-First Scientific Discovery** and candidate Metric/Perspective Generation with explicit validation gates, counterexamples, and independent testing requirements.
- **Delta taxonomy:** World, Observation, Epistemic/Knowledge, and Structural/Operational changes, with separate semantics and evidence.
- **CDE integration:** constrained transitions and policy-driven cycle control; D-BTP-specific No-Delta-Reuse is not imposed as a universal CDE axiom.
- **Self-modeling research path:** WE → Self-Modeling Entity (SME) → candidate Subjective Structural Entity (SSE), without claiming self-modeling demonstrates phenomenal consciousness.
- **Definition Evolution Loop (DEL)** with invariant-based definitions, counterexample-triggered revisions, regression checks, and versioned decisions.
- **SIF localization hypothesis:** WE-HDT and CDE as potentially complementary localization/addressing approaches, pending empirical and architectural validation.

### Changed — from originating research ideas

- Broadened the starting point from variable-size block indexing to entity-centered, multi-perspective structural operations.
- Separated retrieval guarantees from hypothesis-generation mechanisms and from intelligence allocation.
- Treated residuals as evidence-bearing research objects rather than automatically as noise or discoveries.
- Distinguished semantic correctness from performance improvements and architectural ambition from verified capabilities.
- Reframed generality around minimal invariants and conformance, not exhaustive inventories of entities or metrics.

### Explicitly not included / not claimed

- No production-ready WE-HDT, AVIB, MLIU, or autonomous scientific discovery engine.
- No verified speedup over k-d trees, SQL indexes, or other standard baselines.
- No validated new physical law, clinical result, or autonomous research breakthrough.
- No assertion that self-reference or self-modeling establishes subjective experience.
- No universal requirement that CDE forbid repeated deltas or repeated states.
- No guarantee of ten separately exported figure files before a final asset audit.
- No DOI is asserted until a Zenodo or equivalent deposit returns an actual identifier.

## Versioning and maintenance policy

### Proposed version meanings

- **v0.1.x:** editorial corrections, link repairs, metadata fixes, and clarifications that preserve research contracts.
- **v0.2.0:** substantive new or revised research contracts, baseline implementations, additional validation assets, or major architectural refinements.
- **v1.0.0:** a future milestone requiring explicitly stated criteria; not implied by the number of papers or figures alone.

### Recording future changes

For each significant update, record:

1. Date, version, author/contributor, and affected artifacts.
2. Category: Added, Changed, Deprecated, Removed, Fixed, Security, or Evidence Update.
3. The hypothesis, definition, counterexample, decision, or unresolved residual IDs affected.
4. Rationale, alternatives considered, and compatibility implications.
5. Evidence grade, reproducibility information, and negative findings.
6. Required regression tests and any new explicit leftovers.

Never silently overwrite a previously published definition, hypothesis status, or negative result. Use versioned amendments and cross-links to preserve the research trail.

---

**Guiding principle:** *Specifications before runtime; conformance before performance; evidence before promotion; explicit leftovers before premature closure.*
