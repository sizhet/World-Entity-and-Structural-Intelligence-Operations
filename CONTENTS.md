## 6. Research-Trace Workflow

```text
Observation / problem / counterexample
                |
                v
      Unresolved Residual
                |
                v
       Hypothesis Registry
                |
                v
    Conformance / experiment
          /           \
     Supported       Refuted / inconclusive
          \           /
           v         v
     Definition and Decision Review
                |
                v
     Versioned Research Relay
```

Maintain the following distinction in every relay package: **proposed claim ≠ tested claim ≠ independently reproduced claim**. A counterexample is not automatically an empirical refutation unless its assumptions and reproducibility are established.

## 9. Contributing a Research Relay

A useful contribution identifies its target definition or hypothesis, specifies assumptions and evidence provenance, reports conformance or failure cases, and records unresolved leftovers. Negative results and failed replications are first-class research contributions.

**Suggested relay fields:** `relayId`, `sourceDocument`, `claimIds`, `definitionIds`, `counterexampleIds`, `method`, `datasetOrScenario`, `result`, `limitations`, `evidenceLevel`, `nextAction`, `version`, and `provenance`.
