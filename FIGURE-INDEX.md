## 2. Recommended Insertion Locations

Insertion locations are **semantic anchors**: use the named discussion or the closest matching heading in the actual paper. Do not assume a specific section number if headings have changed.

### Fig-001 — WE-SIO Grand Architecture

**File:** `figures/Fig-001-WE-SIO-Grand-Architecture.png`  
**Primary insertion:** `docs/WE-SIO-001-From-Variable-Size-Blocks-to-World-Entity.md`, immediately after the first end-to-end WE-SIO architecture overview.  
**Secondary reference:** `README.md`, in the architecture section; `START-HERE.md`, after the conceptual orientation.

**Intended content:** World entities and typed perspectives feed WE-HDT localization; MLIU and Two-Ways CCC support inference and questions; residual discovery, CDE, definition evolution, and relay are coordinated by contracts and control-plane governance.

**Caption:** *Figure 1. Proposed WE-SIO architecture linking entity representation, hybrid localization, accountable intelligence operations, residual-driven discovery, and collective learning.*

**Boundary:** Arrows denote conceptual or proposed interface relationships, not an implemented dataflow or measured system throughput.

### Fig-002 — From Variable-Size Blocks to World Entity

**File:** `figures/Fig-002-From-Variable-Size-Blocks-to-World-Entity.png`  
**Primary insertion:** `docs/WE-SIO-001-From-Variable-Size-Blocks-to-World-Entity.md`, after the discussion of variable-size blocks and two-phase candidate search.  
**Secondary reference:** `cases/CASE-002-Variable-Size-Blocks-vs-KD-Tree.md`, in the architectural comparison.

**Intended content:** Adaptive blocks and candidate search evolve into an entity-centric abstraction with identity, perspective descriptors, evidence, and multiple addressing strategies.

**Caption:** *Figure 2. Conceptual progression from variable-size search blocks to a typed, multi-perspective World Entity model.*

**Boundary:** Variable-size partitions alone are not established as novel or superior to k-d trees; comparisons require specified algorithms and benchmarks.

### Fig-003 — WE Hybrid Differential Tree

**File:** `figures/Fig-003-WE-Hybrid-Differential-Tree.png`  
**Primary insertion:** `docs/WE-SIO-003-WE-Hybrid-Differential-Tree-Architecture.md`, after the node/block/leaf architecture description.  
**Secondary references:** `specs/SPEC-003-WE-HDT-Node-Block-and-Metric-Leaf-Contract.md`; `extensions/EXT-001-WE-HDT-as-an-SIF-Localization-Pillar.md`.

**Intended content:** Typed candidate localization branches (Euclidean, mathematical metric, similarity, relational/directional) connect to evidence-aware resolution. Logical metric leaves need not correspond to physical storage leaves.

**Caption:** *Figure 3. Proposed WE-HDT: typed perspective-specific localization followed by evidence-aware entity resolution.*

**Boundary:** Euclidean-first traversal is optional; abstract entities may lack Euclidean coordinates. Similarity and directionality are not automatically metrics.

### Fig-004 — Adaptive Variable-Intelligence Blocks

**File:** `figures/Fig-004-Adaptive-Variable-Intelligence-Blocks.png`  
**Primary insertion:** `docs/WE-SIO-004-Adaptive-Variable-Intelligence-Blocks.md`, after the two-axis adaptation model.  
**Secondary reference:** `cases/CASE-002-Variable-Size-Blocks-vs-KD-Tree.md`, in adaptive workload experiments.

**Intended content:** One axis changes block granularity (split/merge/reindex); another changes local intelligence allocation (activate/delegate/deactivate). CDE proposes controlled transitions between configurations.

**Caption:** *Figure 4. Independent but potentially co-evolving partition-granularity and intelligence-capability axes in PAVB/AVIB.*

**Boundary:** Adaptation has overhead; neither finer blocks nor more agents guarantee improvement.

### Fig-005 — Metric Leaf Intelligence Unit

**File:** `figures/Fig-005-Metric-Leaf-Intelligence-Unit.png`  
**Primary insertion:** `docs/WE-SIO-005-Metric-Leaf-Intelligence-and-SI-Operations.md`, after the MLIU minimal contract.  
**Secondary reference:** `specs/SPEC-004-SI-Operations-and-Two-Ways-CCC-Interface.md`.

**Intended content:** Typed leaf references, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, residuals, resource budget, and six operations: Locate, Resolve, Relate, Infer, Question, Evolve.

**Caption:** *Figure 5. MLIU contract and six accountable operations, with provenance, uncertainty, and bounded resource use.*

**Boundary:** A metric leaf is a logical intelligence interface, not necessarily a heavyweight autonomous agent.

### Fig-006 — Two-Ways CCC Cross-Metric Discovery

**File:** `figures/Fig-006-Two-Ways-CCC-Cross-Metric-Discovery.png`  
**Primary insertion:** `docs/WE-SIO-006-Two-Ways-CCC-and-Cross-Metric-Discovery.md`, after forward/reverse workflow definitions.  
**Secondary reference:** `extensions/EXT-003-Structural-Scientific-Discovery-and-Metric-Generation.md`.

**Intended content:** Forward traversal generates candidates and accountable resolutions; reverse traversal uses unresolved residuals to formulate questions and candidate perspectives, with typed bridges and independent validation.

**Caption:** *Figure 6. Two-Ways CCC: forward evidence-constrained resolution and reverse residual-driven question generation.*

**Boundary:** Reverse questions are hypotheses, not proof; cross-perspective mappings must pass semantic and type checks.

### Fig-007 — Residual-Driven Scientific Discovery Loop

**File:** `figures/Fig-007-Residual-Driven-Scientific-Discovery-Loop.png`  
**Primary insertion:** `docs/WE-SIO-007-Residual-First-Scientific-Discovery.md`, after the residual-to-validation loop.  
**Secondary references:** `docs/WE-SIO-008-Metric-Discovery-and-Perspective-Evolution.md`; `cases/CASE-003-Clinical-Treatment-Response-Residuals.md`.

**Intended content:** Prediction → observation → residual → data-quality and confounding checks → hypothesis/perspective candidate → independent evaluation → promote, reject, or retain unresolved.

**Caption:** *Figure 7. Residual-first scientific discovery loop with explicit falsification and evidence-quality gates.*

**Boundary:** A residual indicates an explanatory gap; it is not by itself a new mechanism or scientific law.

### Fig-008 — World Delta vs Epistemic Delta

**File:** `figures/Fig-008-World-Delta-vs-Epistemic-Delta.png`  
**Primary insertion:** `docs/WE-SIO-009-World-Delta-Knowledge-Delta-and-CDE.md`, after the delta taxonomy.  
**Secondary reference:** `cases/CASE-004-Chess-as-a-CDE-Boundary-Stress-Test.md`.

**Intended content:** Separate real-world changes, recorded observations/corrections, belief revisions, and structural/operational modifications; CDE transitions are governed by applicability and state contracts.

**Caption:** *Figure 8. Distinct world, observation, epistemic, and structural delta semantics within constrained evolution.*

**Boundary:** Correcting a record does not reverse the physical world. No-Delta-Reuse is an optional path policy, not a universal CDE axiom.

### Fig-009 — Definition Evolution Loop

**File:** `figures/Fig-009-Definition-Evolution-Loop.png`  
**Primary insertion:** `docs/WE-SIO-011-Minimal-Kernel-Contracts-and-Definition-Evolution.md`, after the Definition Evolution Loop specification.  
**Secondary references:** `research-trace/DEFINITION-REGISTRY.md`; `research-trace/COUNTEREXAMPLE-REGISTRY.md`.

**Intended content:** Minimal kernel → application → counterexample → propose narrow/split/broaden/revert → invariant review → regression tests → versioned decision and relay.

**Caption:** *Figure 9. Counterexample-driven definition evolution with invariant preservation, regression testing, and traceable revision.*

**Boundary:** Definitions may be incomplete without being imprecise; avoid making a definition vacuous to evade counterexamples.

### Fig-010 — From WE to Self-Modeling Entity and Collective Relay

**File:** `figures/Fig-010-From-WE-to-Self-Modeling-Entity-and-Collective-Relay.png`  
**Primary insertion:** `docs/WE-SIO-010-From-World-Entity-to-Self-Modeling-Entity.md`, after the WE → SME → SSE distinction.  
**Secondary references:** `extensions/EXT-004-Self-Referential-World-Entity-and-Subjective-SI.md`; `extensions/EXT-002-Multi-Agent-WE-SI-and-Collective-Learning.md`.

**Intended content:** External entity model → self-observation and calibrated self-model → guarded self-revision → multi-agent evidence/relay; the proposed subjective-structural branch is marked as an open research question.

**Caption:** *Figure 10. Proposed progression from World Entity to self-modeling and collective relay, without equating self-modeling with subjective experience.*

**Boundary:** Self-description and feedback do not establish consciousness; agent agreement is not independent evidence.

- [ ] The image's visible title and diagram semantics agree with the planned filename and caption.

- [ ] Entity identity, representation, and perspective descriptors remain distinct.
- [ ] Mathematical metric, measurement, similarity, and directional relation are visually distinguished.
